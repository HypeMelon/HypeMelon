- **Energy Source**: Glowing watermelon (bright, luminescent, ethereal)

- **Overall Mood**: Visionary genius meets cutting-edge technology

## 🔗 Quality Checklist Before Publishing

- [ ] Resolution: 1600x900 minimum (16:9)
- [ ] Colors: Neon green (#00FF00) prominent, black background
- [ ] Text: Clear, legible, white or neon green, bold font
- [ ] Tesla: If included, portrait or recognizable silhouette
- [ ] Watermelon: Glowing or energy-infused, not realistic/dull
- [ ] Electricity: Visible arcs, glowing lines, or energy effects
- [ ] Contrast: High contrast, not washed out
- [ ] Watermark/Logo: None (unless requested by marketing)
